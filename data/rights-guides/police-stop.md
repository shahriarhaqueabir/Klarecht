# Police Stop — Know Your Rights

## What the police can do

- Ask for your **ID (Ausweis)** — you must show it (§ 111 StPO for suspects, general ID requirement under PersAuswG)
- Ask for your name, address, and date of birth
- Search you or your vehicle **only with legal grounds** (§ 163 StPO, § 34 StPO)

## What you DON'T have to do

- **You do not have to answer questions** beyond identifying yourself. You have the right to remain silent (§ 136 StPO, Art. 2 GG — right to free development of personality includes right to silence)
- You do not have to go to the police station voluntarily
- You do not have to consent to a search of your home or phone

## Key phrases (German)

- "Ich möchte meinen Anwalt sprechen." — I want to speak to my lawyer.
- "Ich mache von meinem Schweigerecht Gebrauch." — I invoke my right to remain silent.
- "Bin ich festgenommen oder frei zu gehen?" — Am I arrested or free to go?

## If arrested

- You have the right to a lawyer (§ 137 StPO)
- You must be brought before a judge within 24 hours (Art. 104 GG)
- You have the right to know the charges against you (§ 136 StPO)

## Important

- Always be polite but firm
- Do not physically resist, even if you believe the search is unlawful — challenge it later
- Note the officers' names/badge numbers if possible
- You can file a complaint (Beschwerde) against unlawful police action

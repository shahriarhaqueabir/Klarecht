# Traffic Stop — Know Your Rights

## What the police can do

- Stop you and check your **driving licence (Führerschein)** and **vehicle registration (Fahrzeugschein)** (StVO, StVG)
- Check insurance (Versicherung) and vehicle condition (roadworthiness)
- Test for alcohol/drugs if there is suspicion (§24a StVG)
- Issue a warning or fine for violations (OWiG)

## What you must show

- Driving licence
- Vehicle registration document
- Proof of insurance (if requested)
- Your ID (§111 StPO)

## What you DON'T have to do

- **You do not have to admit fault** or make statements about the incident
- You do not have to consent to a vehicle search beyond a safety check
- You do not have to pay a fine on the spot — you can object (Einspruch)

## Alcohol and drug tests

- Police may conduct a breath test (Atemalkoholtest) with suspicion
- Refusal can lead to a blood test ordered by a prosecutor
- Legal limits: 0.5‰ (general), 0.0‰ for new drivers/probation (§24a StVG)
- Drug driving is prohibited; testing follows specific procedures

## Fines and points (OWiG, StVG §4)

- Minor offences: warning (Verwarnung) or fine (Bußgeld)
- Points are recorded in Flensburg (Fahreignungsregister)
- You can file an objection (Einspruch) within 2 weeks
- Serious offences may lead to licence suspension or revocation

## Key phrases (German)

- "Ich mache keine Angaben zum Vorfall." — I make no statements about the incident.
- "Ich lege Einspruch ein." — I file an objection.
- "Darf ich meinen Anwalt kontaktieren?" — May I contact my lawyer?

## Important

- Stay calm and polite; do not argue roadside
- Note the officer's details and the exact location/time
- Photograph evidence if safe and lawful
- Deadlines for objections are short (2 weeks) — act promptly
- Consult a traffic lawyer (Verkehrsrecht) for serious matters

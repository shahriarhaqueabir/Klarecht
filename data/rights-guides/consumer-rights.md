# Consumer Rights — Know Your Rights

## Defective goods (BGB §434)

- A product is defective if it lacks the agreed quality or is unfit for normal use
- Defects include physical damage, missing parts, wrong description, or software bugs
- The seller is liable for defects present at delivery (BGB §434)

## Your remedies (BGB §437, §439)

- **Subsequent performance (Nacherfüllung)**: repair or replacement (§439 BGB)
- If repair fails or is unreasonable: withdraw from contract (Rücktritt) or reduce price (Minderung)
- Claim damages for further losses (Schadensersatz)
- Warranty period: **2 years** from delivery (§438 BGB)

## How to claim

- Notify the seller in writing as soon as you discover the defect
- Set a reasonable deadline for repair/replacement
- Keep receipts, photos, and correspondence
- If the seller refuses: escalate to consumer advice centre (Verbraucherzentrale) or court

## Distance selling / online purchases (FernAbsG, BGB §355)

- **14-day withdrawal right** (Widerrufsrecht) for online/phone/mail orders
- No reason needed — you can change your mind
- Return the goods; seller refunds within 14 days
- Exceptions: custom-made goods, sealed hygiene products, digital content started with consent

## Key phrases (German)

- "Ich widerrufe den Kaufvertrag." — I withdraw from the purchase contract.
- "Die Ware ist mangelhaft, ich verlange Nacherfüllung." — The goods are defective; I demand repair/replacement.
- "Ich setze Ihnen eine Frist von 14 Tagen." — I set you a deadline of 14 days.

## Important

- Act within warranty periods — 2 years for new goods
- Withdrawal must be declared clearly (email/letter is fine)
- Keep proof of return shipment
- For digital subscriptions, check cancellation terms (Kündigung) separately
- Contact Verbraucherzentrale for free advice

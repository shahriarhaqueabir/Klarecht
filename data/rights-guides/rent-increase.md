# Rent Increase — Know Your Rights

## When can your landlord raise the rent?

- Only in limited situations defined by law (BGB §558 — Mieterhöhung bis zur ortsüblichen Vergleichsmiete)
- After modernisation work on the building (BGB §559 — Modernisierungsmieterhöhung)
- When operating costs (Nebenkosten) change (BGB §560)
- The rent may not exceed the local comparable rent (ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete)

## Formal requirements (BGB §558)

- The increase must be **in writing** (Schriftform)
- It must state the new rent and the reasons (e.g., reference to a rent index / Mietspiegel)
- You have **2 months** to object (Widerspruch) from receipt
- The increase takes effect at the earliest after the objection period

## What you can do

- Check the rent index (Mietspiegel) for your city
- Object in writing if the increase is too high or unjustified
- Request proof of the modernisation costs (BGB §559)
- Seek advice from a tenants' association (Mieterverein) or a lawyer

## Caps and limits

- Cap of 20% over 3 years in most areas (Kappungsgrenze, §558 BGB)
- In tight housing markets: 15% cap over 3 years (§558 BGB, Mietpreisbremse areas)
- Modernisation increases capped at 8% of costs per year (§559 BGB)

## Key phrases (German)

- "Ich widerspreche der Mieterhöhung." — I object to the rent increase.
- "Bitte senden Sie mir den Mietspiegel." — Please send me the rent index.
- "Ich bitte um eine Aufstellung der Modernisierungskosten." — I request a breakdown of modernisation costs.

## If you face eviction

- Termination requires a legitimate interest (§573 BGB)
- You can challenge an unjustified termination in court (§574 BGB — Widerspruch)
- Seek legal help immediately — deadlines are short

## Important

- Never stop paying rent without legal advice — pay the old rent and dispute the increase
- Deadlines matter: object within 2 months
- Keep all correspondence in writing
- A rent increase does not justify termination without cause (§573 BGB)

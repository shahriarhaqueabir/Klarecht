# Employment Termination — Know Your Rights

## When is a dismissal valid? (KSchG §1)

- Dismissal must be **socially justified** (sozial gerechtfertigt)
- Valid grounds: conduct (Verhalten), personal reasons (personenbedingt), or operational reasons (betriebsbedingt)
- The employer must consider the employee's interests and possible alternatives

## Formal requirements (BGB §623, KSchG §4)

- Termination must be **in writing** (Schriftform) — email/SMS is invalid
- It must be signed by the employer or an authorised person
- Verbal dismissals are void
- The employer must hand over or send the written notice

## Notice periods (BGB §622)

- During probation (Probezeit): 2 weeks
- After probation: at least 4 weeks to the 15th or end of a month
- Longer periods apply with years of service (up to 7 months after 20 years)
- Extraordinary dismissal (fristlos) requires an important reason (§626 BGB)

## Extraordinary dismissal (BGB §626)

- Only for serious cause (wichtiger Grund), e.g., theft, violence, gross misconduct
- Must be declared within 2 weeks of learning of the reason
- Employee may also terminate extraordinarily (e.g., unpaid wages)

## What to do after receiving notice

- **File a dismissal protection claim (Kündigungsschutzklage) within 3 weeks** at the Labour Court (Arbeitsgericht) — KSchG §4
- Missing the deadline usually means accepting the dismissal
- Request a reference (Arbeitszeugnis) and final pay
- Register as jobseeker (Arbeitsuchendmeldung) within 3 days

## Key phrases (German)

- "Ich erhebe Kündigungsschutzklage." — I am filing a dismissal protection claim.
- "Ich bitte um ein qualifiziertes Arbeitszeugnis." — I request a detailed work reference.
- "Wann ist mein letzter Arbeitstag?" — When is my last working day?

## Important

- Act fast — the 3-week court deadline is strict
- Do not sign anything you don't understand (e.g., termination agreements / Aufhebungsvertrag) without advice
- Severance (Abfindung) is often negotiable
- Consult a labour lawyer or union representative
